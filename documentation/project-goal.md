# Project Goal: Breakpoint

## What Breakpoint Is

Breakpoint is a product about helping a person figure out what they want to learn and what they want to do with it.

It is not just about picking a topic.

It is about identifying a concrete thing the user wants to become able to do, then helping them learn toward that.

## Core Goals

Breakpoint has three main goals:

1. Help the user find what they want to learn.
2. Help the user define what they want to do with what they learn.
3. Teach them through a great curriculum and measure whether they are actually getting better.

## Product Shape

The broad shape of the product is:

- the user identifies a learning direction
- the user identifies a concrete applied goal
- the system teaches through a strong curriculum
- the system measures improvement over time

Those are the essentials.

This document should not assume more than that.

## Curriculum

Breakpoint should teach through a great curriculum.

That means the curriculum should be good enough to actually help someone improve, not just expose them to information.

This document does not lock in a specific curriculum format, teaching method, structure, or pedagogy beyond that.

## Measurement

Breakpoint should be able to measure whether a user is getting better.

That measurement is important.

The right metric may be non-obvious.

It may not match standard educational metrics, engagement metrics, or the first metric an AI would suggest.

This document should not prematurely define that metric.

It should only preserve the idea that:

- improvement must be measurable
- the metric matters deeply
- the metric may be unusual or domain-specific
- the product should stay open to discovering the right measurement approach

## Important Constraint

Do not assume product details that have not been explicitly stated.

Do not hard-code:

- a specific onboarding philosophy
- a specific research framing
- a specific motivational theory
- a specific curriculum engine structure
- a specific scoring model
- a specific social, ranking, hiring, or competition layer

Those may exist later, but they are not part of the core definition unless explicitly stated elsewhere.

## Canonical Framing

The safest high-level framing for Breakpoint is:

Breakpoint helps people figure out what they want to learn, define what they want to do with that learning, learn through a strong curriculum, and measure whether they are truly improving.
