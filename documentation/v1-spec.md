# V1 Product Specification

## Purpose

This document exists to keep V1 aligned with the broad definition of Breakpoint.

It should not introduce narrow product assumptions that are not already explicitly accepted.

## V1 Goal

V1 should support the core idea:

- the user figures out what they want to learn
- the user figures out what they want to do with that learning
- the product teaches through a strong curriculum
- the product can begin measuring whether the user is getting better

## What V1 Must Respect

- Do not confuse selecting a topic with defining an actual goal.
- Do not confuse content delivery with real teaching.
- Do not confuse engagement with improvement.
- Do not assume the final improvement metric is already obvious.

## Product Constraint

If a detail has not been explicitly defined, do not hard-code it into the product definition.

That includes:

- rigid onboarding ideology
- rigid curriculum ideology
- rigid scoring ideology
- speculative social or marketplace layers
- overly specific research claims

## Canonical V1 Framing

V1 is the earliest version of a product that helps users identify what they want to learn, identify what they want to do with that learning, learn through a strong curriculum, and start measuring real improvement without prematurely locking in the wrong metric.
