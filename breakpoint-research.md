# Breakpoint Research Notes

## Purpose

This file should preserve only the broad research-relevant understanding of Breakpoint.

It should not pretend the project has already committed to a narrow theory, fixed methodology, or finished metric framework.

## Broad Understanding

Breakpoint is about:

- helping a person figure out what they want to learn
- helping them identify what they want to do with that learning
- teaching through a strong curriculum
- measuring whether they are getting better

## Measurement

The measurement problem is central.

The right metric may not be intuitive.

It may not be a standard educational metric.

It may not be something an AI should confidently invent without evidence.

That is not a gap in the idea. That is part of the project.

## Research Constraint

Do not overstate what is known.

Do not assume:

- a validated competency model
- a final scoring system
- a settled experimental design
- a fixed theory of learning
- a fixed product claim beyond the broad goals above

## Canonical Research Framing

If research framing is needed, the safest version is:

Breakpoint is exploring how to help users decide what to learn, define what they want to do with that learning, learn through a strong curriculum, and eventually measure genuine improvement using a metric that may be non-obvious and must not be prematurely assumed.
