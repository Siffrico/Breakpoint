# Test Curriculum 1 — SAT Reading & Writing Mastery Table

**Convention:** Every subtype's testable unit is called an **insight** (not "concept") — this covers rule-knowledge (e.g. comma splice fix), pure comprehension (e.g. extracting main idea), and reasoning moves (e.g. bridging premise to inference) under one label, so mastery can be scored the same way regardless of how concept-dense the subtype is.

**Mechanistic mastery pattern:** Each subtype carries a subtype-level Elo rating (R). A correct response at difficulty D raises R; an incorrect response lowers R; magnitude scales with the gap between R and D (standard Elo update). "Mastery" = R crosses the Class-3 threshold and holds across a rolling window, at which point item delivery stops raising difficulty and shifts to spaced retest only.

| Type | Subtype | Job | Mastery Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Evidence | 1.1 Identify overarching claim | What is the single overarching argument, stripped of details? | Learner is mastered on the insight "main idea ≠ detail ≠ overreach" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, driven by correct answers on items whose difficulty raises R and incorrect answers (esp. detail-as-claim or overreach traps) lowering R proportionally to the Elo gap. |
| | 1.2 Interpret details/data | What does the text/graph explicitly state about this variable? | Learner is mastered on the insight "answer = paraphrase of stated text/data, never outside knowledge" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with each correct extraction raising R and each outside-knowledge or misread-axis error lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 1.3 Infer unstated assumptions | What must be true for the explicit text to hold together logically? | Learner is mastered on the insight "the correct inference fills the exact premise-to-conclusion gap, no more no less" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct minimal-bridge inferences raising R and over-/under-inference errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| 2. Rhetoric/Structure | 2.1 Determine function of an element | Why did the author put this specific element right here in the argument? | Learner is mastered on the insight "function ≠ content summary — locate role via surrounding transition words" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct role-identification raising R and content-summary-mistaken-for-function errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 2.2 Select context-appropriate vocab | Which word has the exact definition that survives substitution into this sentence? | Learner is mastered on the insight "predict-the-blank before checking answer choices, watch for secondary/idiomatic meanings" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct substitutions raising R and common-meaning-trap or grammatical-mismatch errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 2.3 Evaluate argument structure | What is the macro-architecture of the passage's logic? | Learner is mastered on the insight "track logical turns (but/therefore/for example) to build the passage's argument skeleton" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct structure-mapping raising R and micro-detail-over-macro-structure errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| 3. Revise for flow/synthesis | 3.1 Insert transitions | What is the logical relationship between the sentence before and after the blank? | Learner is mastered on the insight "classify the before/after relationship (agree/contrast/cause-effect) before picking a transition word" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct relationship classification raising R and tone-correct-but-meaning-wrong transition errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 3.2 Combine notes into summary | How do I condense the notes into one sentence meeting the prompt's exact rhetorical goal? | Learner is mastered on the insight "the prompt's stated goal is the sole elimination filter — extra true details are still wrong" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with goal-compliant combinations raising R and goal-ignoring or over-inclusive errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 3.3 Alter structure for emphasis | Which structure places the most important information at the point of maximum emphasis? | Learner is mastered on the insight "end-of-sentence position (and active voice) carries maximum rhetorical weight" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct emphasis-placement choices raising R and concision-over-emphasis errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| 4. Grammar | 4.1 Resolve sentence boundaries | Are independent clauses properly separated, and are dependent clauses properly attached? | Learner is mastered on the insight "count independent clauses first, then match punctuation (FANBOYS/semicolon/comma) to that count" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct boundary fixes raising R and comma-splice or phrase-mistaken-for-clause errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 4.2 Resolve agreement errors | Do subjects, verbs, and pronouns match in number/person, ignoring intervening phrases? | Learner is mastered on the insight "cross out prepositional/intervening phrases to expose the true subject before checking agreement" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct agreement fixes raising R and closest-noun-trap errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | 4.3 Resolve modifier placement | Is the noun immediately following the comma the logical doer of the introductory phrase? | Learner is mastered on the insight "match the intro-phrase's logical doer to the noun right after the comma" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct modifier fixes raising R and sounds-right-but-dangling errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |

## Open item: generation tooling
Subtypes requiring novel problem instances (new passages, new flawed sentences) to properly test insight *application* — rather than recall — need a problem-generation pipeline behind the interface, since a plain chat surface can't reliably author calibrated items on the fly. Not resolved in this table; flagged for a separate design pass.

# Test Curriculum 2 — Frontier Software Engineering Mastery Table

**Convention:** Same as above — every subtype's testable unit is an **insight**, whether it's a procedural checklist (e.g. profiling steps), a design heuristic (e.g. backpressure placement), or a diagnostic instinct (e.g. p99 over average).

**Mechanistic mastery pattern:** Same Elo mechanism — subtype-level rating R rises on correct responses at difficulty D and falls on incorrect ones, scaled by the R-D gap. Mastery = R crosses the Class-3 threshold and holds across a rolling window.

| Type | Subtype | Job | Mastery Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Distributed Backend | A1 Design high-throughput data flows | How do I structure data pipelines to meet strict throughput bounds at scale? | Learner is mastered on the insight "model the system as queues/buffers and place backpressure before the bottleneck" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct async/partitioned designs raising R and sync-call-in-event-loop or missing-backpressure errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | A2 Isolate system failure domains | How do I prevent partial failures from cascading and taking down the entire system? | Learner is mastered on the insight "wrap every cross-service call in a circuit breaker/bulkhead so one failure can't propagate" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct isolation designs raising R and unbounded-retry or shared-failure-domain errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | A3 Optimize storage & query latency | How do I store and retrieve data with minimal resource contention and maximum speed? | Learner is mastered on the insight "diagnose via query plan before touching schema or indexes" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct index/query fixes raising R and blind-schema-change or lock-contention-blind errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| B. User Experience Engineering | B1 Shape intuitive user interactions | How do I translate a user need into a minimal, frictionless interaction sequence? | Learner is mastered on the insight "cut the flow to the fewest steps that still preserve user confidence at each step" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correctly minimized flows raising R and friction-adding or clarity-losing designs lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | B2 Optimize rendering & execution speed | How do I minimize main-thread blocking to achieve 60fps/120fps interactions? | Learner is mastered on the insight "profile before fixing — locate whether the 16ms budget is blown by Scripting, Style, or Layout" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct trace-driven fixes raising R and un-profiled/premature optimization errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | B3 Synchronize distributed client state | How do I keep client state consistent with the server under unreliable network conditions? | Learner is mastered on the insight "pick the conflict-resolution model (CRDT/OT/optimistic-lock) that matches the concurrency shape of the data" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct sync designs raising R and last-write-wins-where-conflicts-matter errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| C. Code Fortification & Diagnosis | C1 Refactor code to reveal intent | How do I restructure existing code so its business intent is obvious and behavior is provably correct? | Learner is mastered on the insight "extract pure functions and name them by intent before changing any behavior" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct behavior-preserving refactors raising R and intent-obscuring or behavior-altering-under-refactor errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |
| | C2 Diagnose performance anomalies | How do I systematically trace a symptom back to its root cause in a complex, live system? | Learner is mastered on the insight "anchor on p99 not average, and ask what changed in the last deploy before forming a hypothesis" when subtype-Elo reaches the Class-3 threshold, with correct root-cause diagnoses raising R and hunch-based-patch or average-latency-blind errors lowering R by the Elo-gap-scaled amount. |

## Open item: generation tooling (software engineering)
Same problem as Curriculum 1, amplified — Class 2/3 items here (e.g. "implement a circuit breaker," "diagnose a planted memory leak") require a runnable environment (real DB, real load generator, real heap dumps) to grade application, not just a chat surface. Flagged, not resolved here.
